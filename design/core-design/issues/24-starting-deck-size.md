# 24 — Settle starting deck size

Type: grilling
Status: closed — folded into 10

Map: [core design map](../map.md)

## Closed 2026-08-25, the day it was created

This ticket was split out of [ticket 10](10-sim-the-resource-economy.md) on the strength of a
simulated finding that starting deck size was the most powerful dial in the game and that ticket 09's
provisional 12–15 was far too small.

**That finding was withdrawn.** The simulator had no exemplar cards to work from, so it invented a
card model, and the deck-size curve it produced was a property of the invention rather than of the
game. `[you]` The numbers are worthless without knowing what the synergies and specifics actually
look like.

With the measurement gone, there is nothing here that ticket 10 was not already holding. Ticket 09
handed starting deck size to ticket 10 in the first place, on the correct reasoning that the number
cannot be picked before it is known what a card does. **The question goes back there**, along with
the parts of it a sim could never have answered on its own — what the number costs in printing and in
table feel, and how much of a starting deck is raw stats.

Kept as a closed ticket rather than deleted, so the record shows the split happened and why it was
undone.
