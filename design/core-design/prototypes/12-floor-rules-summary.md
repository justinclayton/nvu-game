# North vs Up — how to play one floor

One page, enough to run a floor with the [exemplar cards](12-exemplar-card-set.md) on a table.
Assembled from tickets 03, 04, 05, 07, 09, 17, 21 and 22, plus the **Down and last stand ruling of
2026-08-25** `[you]`, which supersedes what 04, 07 and 17 said about reaching zero.

## What you have

Two characters, **Red** and **Gray**. Solo, one person runs both; in co-op, one each. The rules are
identical. Everything is open information.

**Each character has three piles**: a **deck** (face down), a **hand**, and an **exhaust pile** (face
up). **The deck is the character's health.** Its height is the whole health bar; nothing is counted or
tracked.

**Cards leave the deck by being Exhausted.** They do not come back during a floor. There is no
discard pile, and you shuffle mid-floor only when a card or the last stand rule tells you to.

**On the table**: the floor deck (draw pile, **Fled** pile, **Cleared** heap), the **active room
zone**, and a **play zone** split into Red's side and Gray's side. Off to the side: the two reward
pools and the Bad Stuff pool.

## Setting up a floor

Build the floor deck from the pools and shuffle it: **1 Enemy room**, **3 Hazard rooms**, and **Stuff
rooms equal to 10 minus the floor number** — 9 on floor 1, 0 on floor 10. Both characters start with
**empty hands**.

## The turn

A turn belongs to the floor, not to a character. Both characters act throughout, simultaneously, in
whatever order they like.

**1 — Flip.** Turn the top card of the floor deck face up into the active room zone. You see what you
are facing before you spend anything.

- A **Stuff room** has a low threshold and no Flee line, and its threshold is **split per character**:
  *"Power 1: Red takes 1 Good Stuff. Scramble 1: Gray takes 1 Good Stuff."* Each character is measured
  on **their own side of the play zone only**, so you pay for your own item and never for your
  partner's. Some rooms print a richer second tier. What you earn is drawn **face down from the Good
  Stuff pool** at the side of the table and goes to that character's hand with `Hold`; a Down
  character earns nothing. **The room is Cleared either way** — meet nothing and you simply leave with
  nothing — and it never goes to Fled.

**2 — Draw.** Each standing character draws cards from their deck into their hand, one at a time, in
any order, each stopping when they say they are done.

- **You must draw at least 1.** There is no way to sit a turn out.
- **You may not draw *up* while at 5 or more cards in hand.** `Hold` cards count. Stuff shoved into
  your hand by a room ignores the cap.
- **A full hand does not excuse the minimum.** If your hand is full, you still draw your 1 — and that
  card goes **straight to your exhaust pile** instead of into your hand. A full hand costs you a card
  a turn; it does not save you one.
- **The only thing that stops you drawing is an empty deck**, and that is last stand. **Being unable
  to draw is not, by itself, Down.**
- **This is the game's central decision.** Every card drawn is stamina spent whether you use it or
  not, because your whole hand is gone at the end of the turn anyway.

**3 — Play.** Both characters play cards into their side of the play zone, in any order, until they
cannot or choose to stop.

- **To play a card, Exhaust cards from your hand equal to its `Cost`.** Cost is paid in *other* cards
  from that character's own hand. Red's cards never pay for Gray's.
- Played cards sit face up. **Their stats join one shared pool for the turn** — Red's `Power 2` beside
  Gray's `Power 1, Scramble 2` gives the team 3 Power and 2 Scramble.
- **A card whose stat is conditional is recalculated every time the pool is read**, along with
  everything else in the pool. Nothing locks on play.
- **The moment the pool meets an Enemy or Hazard room's threshold, the room is Cleared.** Rooms are
  all-or-nothing; there is no partial progress and nothing banks between turns.
- **Exception, for Hazard rooms with two thresholds:** which tier you got locks in when you *declare
  the play phase over*, not the instant one is met. Play everything you mean to play first.
- **Declining is failing without trying.** Spend nothing and take the Flee line. You still had to draw.

**4 — Cleanup.**

- If the room is still in the active room zone, apply its **Flee line**, then put it in the **Fled**
  pile. Where the line says *1 character*, the team chooses which — no splitting.
- If the room was Cleared, put it in the **Cleared** heap. It never comes back and is never counted.
- **Exhaust both hands and the whole play zone**, each character's cards to their own exhaust pile.
  **`Hold` cards in hand are the only exception** — they stay. **Stuff goes to the exhaust pile of
  whoever played it.**
- If the floor draw pile is empty, shuffle the **Fled** pile back into it.

**5 — Turn ends.** Flip again.

## Winning the floor

**Clearing the Enemy room ends the floor.** Hazard and Stuff rooms are resources on the way to the
fight — a floor can end with either still sitting in the deck. Expect to meet the Enemy room, be
unable to afford it, and pay the Flee cost the first time; it comes back around.

## Running out: last stand, and going Down

**When a character's deck is empty, that character is in last stand.** It is a state, not a one-turn
window: they are in it for exactly as long as their deck is empty.

**While in last stand, every card in that character's hand can be played at no cost.** Their partner
pays costs normally.

**A character goes Down when either of these happens:**

- **something would Exhaust a card from their empty deck** — a room's Flee line, a card's own printed
  cost in deck, anything at all; or
- **the team Flees the room while that character is in last stand.** In last stand you have to keep
  clearing rooms. Walking away is what finishes you.

**Getting out of last stand.** If the room is Cleared while a character is in last stand, then at that
cleanup the cards that would have been exhausted from their hand and play zone are **shuffled back
into their deck instead, minus 2, which are exhausted as the price of getting out.** `Hold` cards stay
in hand and are not part of the shuffle-back. Their deck is no longer empty, so they are no longer in
last stand.

**While a character is Down**, they cannot act, cannot draw, cannot play cards, take no rewards, and
**cannot have cards put into their hand** — Stuff cannot be parked on them. Flee lines still land on
the team, and with nobody to share them **every one falls on the survivor**. **The run ends when both
characters are Down.**

## Ascending

- **Pull all Stuff out of both exhaust piles and set it aside.** Stuff does not carry to the next
  floor unless a card says so.
- **Shuffle each exhaust pile back into its deck.** Both characters are now at full health, including
  one who was Down. Ascending is a full heal and a clean slate; nothing bad crosses a floor boundary.
- **Each character is offered 3 cards from their own reward pool and takes one, or declines.** A taken
  card is shuffled into the deck. **A declined card goes to the bottom of its pool.** Declining is a
  real play: a card you skip is consistency you keep.
- Build the next floor's deck.

## The thing to understand before you start

**Your deck is your stamina, so adding a card makes you last longer and makes you worse.** You get
exactly one pass through your deck per floor, so every mediocre card you pick up is a card that turns
up instead of the one you needed. Nothing in the rules stops you from taking every reward. It just
does not go well.

**And your permanent deck is mostly modifiers.** The raw numbers come from Good Stuff, scavenged
fresh every floor and gone when you ascend — and there is less of it on every floor you climb.

## Still unstated, and a table will ask

- **Starting deck size and composition.** Ticket 09 left the number to ticket 10 and it is genuinely
  not decided. For the prototype, use **12 cards each** — Red: 7 Shove, 5 Charge In; Gray: 7 Duck
  Under, 5 Pick The Lock — and treat that as scaffolding, not a decision.
- **What happens to cards left in hand when the floor ends.** Ascending collects the exhaust piles and
  never mentions hands. Affects `Both Barrels`, `Pack Rat`, and any held Stuff.
- **Which character's reward pool a room's reveal line draws from**, and whose deck it goes on top of.
  The printed line says "your deck" and the game has two.
- **Whether a skipped reveal goes to the bottom of its pool**, as a declined ascension reward does.
