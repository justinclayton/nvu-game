# Playtest 12: Seed 12

Recorded 2026-09-30. Rules version 0.2.5 (main at 2a01755). Played by an agent standing in for a
human playtester, in the CLI (`bin/nvu`), seed 12, one shell call per decision. The run ended in
**Victory** on turn 35, when The Monolith Core fell to Oomph 10 / Scramble 10. Red finished with 22
cards Exhausted and 19 live, Gray with 17 Exhausted and 26 live.

This is the first playtest numbered to match its seed. It is a once-over of the current build
before a physical playtest, not a test of one rule.

Notes are the agent's, verbatim, tagged `[agent]`, in the order they were written into the run.
Nothing here is a ruling. Suspected bugs were checked against `rulebook.md` and
`cards.yaml` only; the engine was not read. Where a turn looked unreachable I searched both
hands with a throwaway brute-force script (outside the repo, built from the printed card stats).
That script never read `app/src`, but it means several Band 3 clears below were found by search,
not by eye.

## How the run went

Band 1 (turns 1-15) cost nothing. Floor 1 took six turns: the Sentry Drone came last of the six
rooms I flipped, after three Flooded Ventilation Shafts (Good Stuff each time) and two Turnstiles. Floor 2 was four Security Turnstiles in a row, each cleared on Scramble 3 by Gray alone,
with Red holding the same five cards (note 2). Floor 3 was three Turnstiles and the Drone. No Flee
and no Exhaust on either side (note 3).

Band 2 (turns 16-25) was also clean. Riot Shield carried the Turret's Scramble 8 again. Gears &
Glitch came last on floor 4, fifth on floor 5, and first on floor 6, so floor 6 took one turn. The
only Exhaust was my own: Red's Reckless Swing took a Pry Bar off the deck (note 5).

Band 3 (turns 26-35) is where the run was decided, and it cost four Flees.

- Floor 7 (turns 26-29). Laser Grid came first and was out of reach on both lines, so I Fled (note
  6). The Iron Sentinel came next and was one Scramble short, so I Fled again and took Panic and
  Sluggish (note 7). The Bio-Hazard Vault then Cleared at Oomph 15, with both Bad Stuff cards used
  as payers so their penalties were gone when the room resolved (note 8). The Sentinel came back and
  fell at 10/12 (note 9).
- Floor 8 (turn 30). The Sentinel came first again. Hand by hand it was out of reach, and Gray's
  Hack the Doors showed Red's top three cards. I put Tag Team on top and Rhythm & Bruise drew it
  for an exact 10/10 (note 10).
- Floor 9 (turns 31-33). The Sentinel came first again, unreachable, and I Fled (note 11). The
  Smoldering Armory cleared on Oomph 10 with Bad Stuff as payers. The Sentinel was the last card in
  the floor deck, so it came straight back, and Stim Pack's draw turned a 10/9 hand into 10/12
  (note 12).
- Floor 10 (turns 34-35). Monolith Core's 10/12 (Panic's +2) was out of reach. I made a mistake in
  the plan for turn 34 (note 13) and Fled with Exhaust 5 each. The second try cleared at exactly
  10/10 (note 14).

## What the run showed

1. **Band 3 was a sequence of exact-fit turns, and most needed a search to find.** The Sentinel
   fell three times, the Vault once, and the Monolith once, each at exactly the threshold or within
   two points. Four of those clears came from a scratch-script search (notes 9, 10, 12, 14), not
   from reading the hand. A human at a table would find some of these and Flee the others. Question
   for the designer: is that the intended Band 3 feel, given the playtest is meant to reach a
   physical table?
2. **Bad Stuff is a payment-slot tax, not damage.** Panic, Sluggish and My Head Is Quantum Spinning
   each cost one payment slot on the turn I spent them and did nothing else (note 8, note 12). The
   real cost was the Flee that delivered them: Exhaust 4 or 5 each. Question for the designer:
   is that the intended weight of a Bad Stuff card?
3. **Stairwell position set the run again, with a looping Flee.** The Drone ended floors 1, 2 and 3 on
   turns 6, 11 and 15, while the Sentinel came first on floors 8 and 9 and second on floor 7. On floors 9 and 10 the stairwell was the last card, so a Flee put it straight back and
   made each attempt a fresh full Flee (note 12, note 13). Band 1 and Band 2 cost no Exhaust in
   25 turns; Band 3 cost 34 cards in four Flees.

## The CLI as a playtest tool

Friction, worst first:

1. The moves list after every play prints every payment combination as its own line. I piped
   every call through `grep -v '^card '`, as in playtest 11.
2. There is no way to ask "can this hand reach the threshold?". In Band 3 I wrote a throwaway
   script to search both hands (notes 6, 7, 9, 11, 14). A helper that listed the best reachable
   Oomph and Scramble would have replaced it. It would also need the draws (Stim Pack,
   Rhythm & Bruise, Hack the Doors), which my script did not model, and that is how note 13 happened.
3. `play card` prints the full table after every play. For a five-play turn I needed the stat
   pool line and any draws.
4. The stat pool after Outcome drops a returned Riot Shield's stats (note 9), so the table no
   longer shows what cleared the room.
5. A note's log line is not printed when it is written. I found each by running `replay` and
   grepping for `NOTE`.
6. No live-card count per character (deck plus discard plus hand). I summed by hand at each Band
   boundary, and note 5's count was off.

What worked: staged choices (Good Stuff, card rewards, Hack the Doors' deck pick, Momentum Shift's
Exhaust) each blocked the run and said what they waited on. `play order` accepted the new top of
Red's deck and showed the result. The moves list showed `cost 0` on Red's cards after Gray's
Distract & Pivot, and `cost 0 (printed 1)` for Fast Follow, which settled cost questions before I
committed. `play pile` was the right size for checking a hand mid-turn.

## Candidate issues

Engine mismatches (possible):

- Riot Shield returns to hand during Outcome, before the Good Stuff reveal and before Cleanup, and
  the stat pool line after Outcome drops its Scramble. Seen again on floors 4 and 7 (note 9;
  playtest 11, note 2).

Rulebook questions:

- Is a reward or Good Stuff spread that shows two copies of the same card intended (note 1)?
- Is paying for a card with a Bad Stuff card, to discard it and end its Holding effect, intended
  (note 8)?
- What is "the end of the turn" for Riot Shield: Cleanup, and before or after Discard your play
  zone (playtest 11, note 2)? Still open.

Shape, independent of tuning:

- Band 3 turns are exact-fit puzzles that needed a search (What the run showed, 1).
- When the stairwell is the last card in the floor deck, a Flee returns it as the next flip
  (notes 12, 13).
- Floor 2's Security Turnstile line let Gray clear four turns while Red played nothing (note 2).
- Band 1 and Band 2 cost no Exhaust (note 3, note 5).

Card observations for a later re-tune:

- Hack the Doors, Stim Pack, Rhythm & Bruise and Covering Fire each decided a Band 3 turn (notes
  10, 12, 13). The top-of-deck and draw cards are the strongest levers in the run.
- Overcharged Battery into Distract & Pivot was the 10/10 Sentinel line again (note 9).
- Reckless Swing's random Exhaust cost a Pry Bar in Band 2 (note 5).
- Riot Shield again carried Band 2's Scramble.

CLI:

- Collapse the payment combinations in the moves list (friction 1).
- A reach helper that knows about draws and peeks (friction 2).
- Keep a returned card's stats in the post-Outcome stat pool (friction 4).
- Print a note's log line when it is written (friction 5).
- Print a live-card total per character (friction 6).
