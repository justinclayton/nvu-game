# Playtest 13: Seed 13

Recorded 2026-10-03. Rules version 0.2.9 (main at 0c440de). Played by an agent standing in for a
human playtester, in the CLI (`bin/nvu`), seed 13, one shell call per decision. The run ended in
**Defeat** on turn 5 of floor 1: Gray went Down at the Turn Start draw with 4 live cards. Red
finished with 12 live cards and 0 Exhausted. Gray finished with 4 live and 8 Exhausted.

Notes are the agent's, verbatim, tagged `[agent]`, in the order they were written into the run.
Nothing here is a ruling. Suspected bugs were checked against `design/rulebook.md` and
`design/cards.yaml` only; the engine was not read. One stray test note I wrote by mistake ("x")
was removed from the run file by hand before saving; the replay is unaffected.

## How the run went

Floor 1 opened on three Security Turnstiles in a row, then a Flooded Ventilation Shaft, then a
fourth Turnstile. Red drew Charge In x2, Overdrive and Shove x2 on turn 1 and never played a card.
Gray drew Pick The Lock x3, Peek Around Corner and Duck Under.

Every turn I cleared on Scramble 3 or 4 with Gray alone, using Pick The Lock (Scramble 4, cost 2)
paid by the two weakest cards in hand. That Exhausted 2 Gray cards a turn: 2, 4, 6, 8 after four
turns. Red's five-card hand sat untouched, because the Turnstile's Oomph 5 line needs Red to spend
Charge In plus a payer and also Exhausts one card from each player. Gray's live count was 12, 10, 8,
6, 4. On turn 5 the flip needed three draws and Gray had two cards left across deck and discard.
Gray went Down. I did not see it coming from the table (note 3). I could have asked Red to carry
the Scramble-free lines, but no card lets Red pay for Gray, and Gray was the only one with Scramble.

## Notes

1. **Floor 1, turn 2.** `[agent]` (log 23)

   > Floor 1 turn 2: second Security Turnstile in a row, Gray has the same hand shape. Cheapest Scramble 3 is Pick The Lock (S4) paid by Peek + Duck Under (S1 and S2, cost 1 each, also 2 cards exhausted). Every payment is an Exhaust now, so Scramble 3 costs 2 cards of 12 on turn 1 of the game.

   Rulebook, Play: "pay for it: look at its `Cost`, then move that number of cards from your hand to
   your Exhaust pile." Matches. The cheapest clear of a Scramble 3 line cost 2 cards no matter
   how I built it: Pick The Lock (cost 2), or Peek (cost 1) plus Duck Under (cost 1).

2. **Gray drains while Red idles.** `[agent]` (log 46)

   > Turn 4: Gray is at 6 live after 4 turns (Exhaust 6) while Red has played nothing and holds a full unchanged hand. Gray pays 2 cards per Pick The Lock to clear Scramble 3-4 rooms; since payment now Exhausts, the deck is the HP bar and Gray's bar drains 2 per turn on cheap rooms. Is that the intended pace for Band 1?

   Shape and tuning. Four of the five rooms I met could be cleared on Scramble alone, and Red has
   no Scramble in the starter hand I drew. The Oomph lines on those rooms are priced against
   Red's payers and add an Exhaust 1 to each player. Question for the designer: should both
   characters be expected to share the cost of a floor, and if so what steers the players there?

3. **Down on a short draw.** `[agent]` (log 63)

   > Run over on turn 5: Gray went Down at the Turn Start draw with 4 live cards (hand 2, deck 1, discard 1) because drawing up to five needed 3 cards and only 2 existed. Rulebook sec 9 reads literally this way ('must draw ... deck and discard both empty'), but it means a character with fewer than 5 live cards is Down at the next flip, so the real Stamina is live minus 5. Question: is that intended? Also I never saw this coming from the table, which prints 'live 4' as if it were safe.

   Rulebook, Turn Start: "draws cards from their deck until they hold 5", and section 9: "If you must
   draw or Exhaust a card and your deck and discard pile are both empty, you go Down". The engine
   did this. The Intro says "If you need a card and have none left, you go Down", which reads as
   "you need a card to pay or play", not "you draw a fifth". Questions for the designer: is Down
   at Turn Start with fewer than 5 live cards intended? If so, should the rulebook say so in the
   Intro (Stamina is live cards minus 5)? And should the CLI table mark a character with fewer
   than 6 live cards as in danger? Rule 0.2.9 line to read: "Your deck is your HP total, called
   `Stamina`."

## What the run showed

1. **The run can be lost on floor 1 with a legal, sensible line.** Gray cleared four rooms on
   Scramble alone and died on the fifth turn. Paying a Cost now Exhausts, so Gray's cheap clears
   drain 2 cards per turn, and the room count (10 on floor 1) is far above Gray's 12 cards.
2. **The Down threshold is 5 higher than it looks.** Fewer than 5 live cards at Turn Start loses
   the game (note 3). The table prints "live 4" and no warning.
3. **Red's Oomph cards were never needed.** Four Scramble-first rooms left Red with nothing to do
   for the whole run (note 2). This is the same finding as playtest 12, note 2, but now it kills Gray.

## The CLI as a playtest tool

Friction, worst first:

1. The moves list after every play prints every payment combination as its own line. I piped
   every call through `grep -v '^card '`, as in playtests 11 and 12.
2. A note typed to test the tool ("x") cannot be removed: `play undo` says "Nothing to undo: the
   last move revealed a card" and a note is not an undoable move. I edited the run file by hand.
3. No warning when a character is about to Down at Turn Start. The table shows "live N" but not
   "live N, needs 5 to draw".
4. A note's log line is not printed when it is written. The `at` index in the run file is one less
   than the numbered line `replay` prints, so I cite the `at` value.
5. `play card` prints the table after every play; I needed only the stat pool line.

What worked: the refusal-free flow made a five-turn run take 13 commands. The Down message named
the cause ("drew from an empty deck and discard pile"). The draw lines showed the discard
reshuffle ("the discard pile shuffles in to make a new one (1 cards)"), which let me count the
cards left. `play note` stored the text verbatim.

## Candidate issues

Engine mismatches (possible):

- None seen. The Down on a short draw matches rulebook section 9 as written (note 3).

Rulebook questions:

- Is Down at Turn Start with fewer than 5 live cards intended (note 3)?
- Should the Intro's "if you need a card and have none left" say that a short draw counts?

Shape, independent of tuning:

- Gray can lose on floor 1 by clearing Scramble rooms alone (note 2, What the run showed 1).
- Red can idle for the whole floor while Gray pays every turn (note 2).

Card observations for a later re-tune:

- Pick The Lock is the cheapest Scramble per card exhausted for Gray, so a hand of three of them
  made Scramble-only play the default. Security Turnstile's Scramble 3 line needed 2 cards.

CLI:

- Collapse the payment combinations in the moves list (friction 1).
- Let `undo` remove the last note, or add `play note --drop` (friction 2).
- Warn when a character has fewer than 6 live cards (friction 3).
