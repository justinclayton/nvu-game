# Playtest 13: Seed 32001

Recorded 2026-10-05. Kit revision R29.C45-v2, branch `claude/issue-321` at `fab921f` (the top layer of
the stack, which includes the Catch Your Breath cost change). Played by an agent standing in for a
human playtester, in the CLI (`bin/nvu`), seed 32001, one shell call per decision, no scratch
script. The run ended in **Victory** on turn 38, when The Monolith Core fell to Oomph 10 / Scramble
10. There was no Flee. Red finished with 39 cards Exhausted and 8 live, Gray with 36 Exhausted and 14
live.

Notes are the agent's, verbatim, tagged `[agent]`, in the order they were written into the run.
Nothing here is a ruling. Suspected bugs were checked against `rulebook.md` and `cards.yaml` (through
`bin/nvu card`) only; the engine was not read.

## How the run went

Floor 1 (turns 1-8) was Turnstiles, two Ventilation Shafts and the Sentry Drone last. Red had nothing
to play on turns 1, 2 and 4, because Gray's Pick The Lock or Coil Of Cable cleared Scramble 3 alone. The
cost fell on Gray: Pick The Lock costs 2, and Gray was at 3 live cards after turn 6. A Pair Of
Stitch-Em-Ups (Red's Good Stuff pick on turn 6) moved two of Gray's Pick The Locks back out of the
Exhaust pile on turn 7. The Drone fell at Oomph 4 / Scramble 4 for four Good Stuff cards. Ascending
did not heal anyone (notes 1 and 2).

Floor 2 (turns 9-17) was the same shape with Coil Of Cable. Coil costs 0 and goes to the discard
pile, so Gray cleared two Turnstile turns for nothing. Overcharged Battery into a free Tag Team,
Pocket Dynamo and Riot Shield did the rest. Floor 3 ended on turn 19, two turns in: the Drone
was the second room. Oomph 6 (Pry Bar and Overdrive, both free to play) and Oomph 4 / Scramble 4 were
both met, and both outcomes resolved.

Floor 4 (turns 20-25) was the first floor with real choices: the Turret's Scramble 8, then two
Pressurized Maintenance Hubs (Oomph 7 and Scramble 7, both met each time for two card rewards), two
Hydroponics Bays at Scramble 11 (Set 'Em Up counting Gray's free plays did most of it) and Gears &
Glitch. I Know Kung Fu did not draw on a Scramble card (note 3). High-Frequency Scanner showed the
next three rooms, which let me plan the floor. Hack the Doors looked at the Good Stuff pool (note 4).

Floor 5 (turns 26-30) was a Hydroponics Bay at Scramble 11, three Turrets at Scramble 8 and Gears &
Glitch. Gray's free plays (Coil, Crowbar, Stim Pack, Pocket Dynamo, Pry Bar) did most of the work,
and Red's Set 'Em Up or Riot Shield made up the rest. Floor 6 took one turn: Gears & Glitch came
first and fell at 7/7 with Pry Bar, Fast Follow (free after Gray played), Pocket Dynamo, Crowbar and
two Riot Shields.

Floor 7 (turns 32-34) had the Bio-Hazard Vault, cleared at Scramble 15 without scrapping anything
(Gray's Coils, Set 'Em Up counting six Gray plays), the Smoldering Armory (Oomph 16 for both card
rewards and Good Stuff) and The Iron Sentinel at 10/10. Red was down to 6 live cards that turn.
Floors 8 and 9 (turns 35-37) were the Sentinel twice, with the Laser Grid between them. The Laser
Grid fell at Oomph 18 for Exhaust 2 each; Rhythm & Bruise counting Gray's plays decided it. Covering
Fire, Pocket Dynamo and Stim Pack refilled Gray's hand mid-turn on both Sentinel turns. Red reached 3
live cards on turn 37 and finished the floor with Overcharged Battery, two Tag Teams and Pocket
Dynamo. Floor 10 (turn 38) was one turn: Coil, two Fast Follows, two Pocket Dynamos, a second Coil
and Tag Team for 10/10.

   Rulebook, section 10: "Heal and Reset: Shuffle ALL your cards into your deck, including your hand,
   discard pile, AND exhaust pile." The Exhaust pile line in Table Layout reads the same way: "These
   cards will not reenter your deck until you complete each Floor." The transcript (log 136 to 141)
   shows only "Red shuffles 4 card(s) back in" and the table afterwards still had the Exhaust piles
   (Red 5, Gray 9) and the discard piles. I saw the same at every later Ascend (note 2; log 308,
   559, 713, 753, 883, 934, 1023), and the run ended with Red at 39 Exhausted and Gray at 36. Question
   for the designer: is the Ascend reset in section 10 still the rule? A Pair Of Stitch-Em-Ups,
   Second Wind and Hit 'n Run each move a card out of the Exhaust pile, and I leaned on Stitch-Em-Ups
   five times; those cards would do nothing if every Ascend gave the whole pile back. That suggests
   the engine may be right and the rulebook stale, but the rulebook does not say so.

   would be a clear choice. Question for the designer: is the discard step meant to exist in the
   CLI, and what does "discard" mean for a card from a Stuff pool?

## What the run showed

1. **With no Ascend reset, the run is a slow bleed paid for in payers, and I never Fled.** Red went
   from 12 to 47 cards in total (live plus Exhausted) and finished with 8 live. Gray finished with
   14 live. The pressure was every payer and every Overdrive: both were Exhausted, not discarded. Red
   hit 6 live on turn 34 and 3 live on turn 37 and still won. Whether this is the intended game turns
   on note 1. If the Ascend reset in the rulebook is the rule, this run was much harder than the
   game is meant to be, and a Flee (Exhaust 3 to 5 each) would never have been a real option.
2. **Zero-cost Good Stuff and draw cards carried the run.** Coil Of Cable, Pry Bar, Stim Pack,
   Pocket Dynamo and Crowbar cost 0 and go to the discard pile when played, so they come back at the
   next reshuffle and cost nothing. Floor 3 took 2 turns, floor 6 took 1 and floor 8 took 1,
   because the stairwell came early. Floors 2 and 5 were cleared mostly by Gray's Coils and Riot
   Shields. Early in the run (turns 1 to 10) Red played nothing on five turns.
3. **Triggered cards read the same and behave differently.** Covering Fire, Crowbar (extra Good
   Stuff, seen on turns 26, 27 and 29), Pocket Dynamo and Tag Team all triggered. I Know Kung Fu did
   not (note 3). That is the only card in this run that did not do what its text said, as far as I
   could tell.

## The CLI as a playtest tool

Friction, worst first:

1. The moves list after every play prints every payment combination as its own line. For a hand of
   five it is 5 to 8 lines, repeated after each card. I had no way to filter it, because I was not
   to pipe output. The list was longer than the event line in most calls.
2. The room's own text is not printed on the table. The Bio-Hazard Vault's rule ("Players may Scrap
   Good Stuff cards ... +3 Oomph or +3 Scramble per card") is printed by `bin/nvu card` and by
   `cards.yaml`, but the table showed only the thresholds. I found out about the rule because a
   `scrap Red Overcharged Battery for <Oomph|Scramble>` line appeared in the moves list.
3. A note's log line is not printed when it is written. I found each by running `replay`.
4. The Battery discount shows as `cost 0` on both players' cards in the moves list, but nothing
   says it ends after one card. I spent a free play on Tag Team (turn 33) when Gray's Pivot, at
   cost 2, would have saved more. A line such as "next play free" in the table would have helped.
5. A staged `take` (the card reward) takes two calls and shows "staged, not yet played"; a mistyped
   second `take` prints the legal names, which was enough to recover.

What worked: `bin/nvu card` for every unfamiliar card face before choosing; staged choices (Good
Stuff, rewards, Peek order, Momentum Shift's Exhaust) that blocked the run and said what they waited
on; `Covering Fire: Red played Overcharged Battery. Draw 1 card?`, which named the trigger; `cost 0
(printed 1)` on Fast Follow and the discounted cards; the table's `live` count per character; and
`replay`, which matched the run.

## Candidate issues

Engine mismatches (possible):

- Ascend does not shuffle the Exhaust pile or the discard pile into the deck, against rulebook
  section 10 (notes 1, 2).
- I Know Kung Fu did not offer a draw on Scramble cards (note 3).
- Peek offered no discard step (note 4).

Rulebook questions:

- Is the section 10 reset still the rule, given Stitch-Em-Ups, Second Wind and Hit 'n Run (notes
  1, 2)?
- What is a "Scramble card" (note 3)?
- What does "discard" mean for a card Peeked from a Stuff pool (note 4)?
- Does a Battery or Pivot discount apply to the partner's next card, as the moves list showed
  (cost 0 on Gray's cards after Red's Battery)? I only used it on the player who played it.

Shape, independent of tuning:

- Red had nothing to play on five of the first ten turns (Gray cleared Scramble 3 alone).
- When the stairwell comes in the first or second flip, a floor takes 1 or 2 turns (floors 3, 6, 8).
- Zero-cost cards that return to the discard pile are free every turn, so only paying cards and
  Exhaust effects cost anything.

Card observations for a later re-tune:

- Catch Your Breath (cost 2, Scramble 2, Peek 3) was offered four times. I took it once and used it
  once, as a payer, and never played it. Hack the Doors (cost 1, Scramble 3, Peek 3) was strictly
  better. This is the card the branch changed, and at cost 2 I never wanted to play it.
- Overdrive's "Exhaust 1" hit a Stitch-Em-Ups, an Overcharged Battery and a Pry Bar on different
  turns. Effectively it costs a random card.
- Set 'Em Up (Scramble per Gray card) and Rhythm & Bruise (Oomph per Gray card) each decided
  turns once Gray had five or six free plays.
- Zen Mode was taken and drawn once but never played; it is untested here.
- Hit 'n Run was taken and drawn but never played.

CLI:

- Collapse the payment combinations in the moves list (friction 1).
- Print room text on the table (friction 2).
- Print a note's log line when it is written (friction 3).
