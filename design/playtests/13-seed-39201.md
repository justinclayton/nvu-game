# Playtest 13: Seed 39201

Recorded 2026-10-26. Kit revision R34.C58-v17 (rules R34; branch `claude/mystifying-volhard-3b8ec6` at
`db0534f9`). Played by an agent standing in for a human playtester, in the CLI (`bin/nvu`), seed
39201, one shell call per decision. The run ended in **Defeat** on turn 8, on floor 2, at Turn Start,
when Corrosive Acid made Red go Down. Red had 10 cards Exhausted and 8 live, Gray 10 Exhausted and 6
live.

Notes are the agent's, verbatim, tagged `[agent]`, in the order they were written into the run.
Nothing here is a ruling. Suspected bugs were checked against `rulebook.md` and `cards.yaml` only;
the engine was not read.

## How the run went

Floor 1 took two turns and cost nothing that lasted. Ventilation Shaft came first and cleared on
Oomph 4 and Scramble 4 (Charge and Pick The Lock, each paid with the cheap cards). The Sentry Drone
came second. Neither hand held Scramble 8, so I went for Oomph 8: Overdrive (which Exhausted a Charge
off Red's deck), Charge paid by two Shoves, and Gray's Work The Angles for its Oomph 1. I took Tag Team
and Hit 'n Run after the Shaft, and Second Wind and In Step after the Drone. Everything Exhausted
healed on Ascend (note 1).

Floor 2 is where the run was lost, and it was lost on turn 4. The floor deck was Maintenance, then
Gears & Glitch, a stairwell. Maintenance carried Cap 3. Gray's best was Scramble 6, so only Red played
and took Oomph 7 with Overdrive and Charge (note 2). Gears & Glitch needed Oomph 7 and Scramble 7 or
Oomph 12. By hand, Red reached Oomph 7 with no Scramble, and Gray reached Scramble 6. I could not
find a split that met both, so I Fled without playing (note 3). That cost Exhaust 3 each and gave
Red Faceful Of Slime and Gray Spore Cloud.

Spore Cloud made Gray discard down to 3 cards at Cleanup. The prompt was worded so that I discarded
the cards I meant to keep (note 4). On turn 5 Hydroponics Bay needed Scramble 11 and I paid 3 cards
to play and Scrap Spore Cloud. Then I Fled anyway, and Red got Panic and Gray got Slime. On turn 6
Room 1-3 was unreachable, because Gray held two Pick The Lock cards and a Slime, and only one card
could pay (note 5). On turn 7 the Turret was also out of reach (note 6). Each Flee exhausted two more
cards from each deck and added more Bad Stuff.

On turn 8 Red's deck and discard were both empty, with eight cards in hand and Corrosive Acid among
them. At Turn Start the Acid's "Exhaust 1" ended the run (note 7).

7. **Corrosive Acid and Down.** `[agent]` (log 207)

   > Turn 8 Turn Start: Corrosive Acid ('At Turn Start, Exhaust 1') with Red's deck and discard both empty ended the run as Defeat while Red held 8 cards in hand. Rulebook section 9: 'If one player has no cards in their hand, deck, or discard pile, you go Down.' Red had a full hand, so I expected no Down.    `cards.yaml`, Corrosive Acid: "Holding: At Turn Start, Exhaust 1." The rulebook says `Exhaust X` is
   "the same as `Exhaust X cards from your deck`" and that an empty deck reshuffles the discard pile.
   Red's discard was empty too, so there was nothing to exhaust. The log then reads "Red is Down",
   discards all eight cards from Red's hand, and prints "You lose." Section 9 says Down is when a
   player has no cards in hand, deck, or discard pile. Red had eight in hand, so I think this
   breaks section 9. Two readings could explain it: the engine treats an unpayable Exhaust as Down,
   or the designer meant that. The rulebook is silent. Question for the designer: what should an
   Exhaust do when the deck and discard are both empty?

   Also in the log: before Down, the Acid's trigger discarded Red's hand. The rulebook gives no
   reason to discard on Down.

## What the run showed

1. **One unreachable stairwell on turn 4 decided the run.** Gears & Glitch needed Oomph 7 and
   Scramble 7, and neither hand had enough payers for a split. Every later problem followed from the
   Bad Stuff that Flee delivered. Two floors earlier, floor 1 took two turns. Floor 2's draw was
   the opposite, a stairwell early and no way to meet it.
2. **Bad Stuff compounds through the hand.** Slime, Spore Cloud, Sluggish and Panic each reduce what a
   hand can do, and none of them can pay. A second Flee did not fix a hand; it added a card to it.
   By turn 7 neither player could reach any room, and I could not undo it by playing (a Slime costs 3
   to play and a Spore Cloud costs 3). Question for the designer: is Flee meant to be recoverable?
3. **Down is reached with a full hand.** Whether this is an engine mismatch or the intended meaning
   of a failed Exhaust, a player with eight cards was declared lost (note 7).

## The CLI as a playtest tool

Friction, worst first:

1. The discard prompt in note 4 says "Discard down to 3 cards" and then "choose 3 of", which reads as
   a choice of cards to keep. The prompt should say "choose 3 to discard".
2. I had no way to ask whether a hand can pay for a play. For Gears & Glitch and the Turret I worked
   out payer counts by hand. The moves list prints a line for each payment combination, so a hand
   with a Slime and a Sluggish in it, where nothing at all is playable, shows no move and no reason.
3. `play note` prints the note text back but not its log line. I found the lines with `replay`.
4. The room faces print `Cap` and `Dim` with no hint at the rule. I had to read `cards.yaml` and
   `rulebook.md` for them, and the rulebook has neither.
5. The `live` figure in the table counts deck, discard and hand. I could not see at a glance how many
   cards were in play at each Flee.

What worked: `play card` printed only what changed. The "cost 3 (printed 2)" form after Sluggish
settled a cost question before I committed. `bin/nvu card` gave a card's face in one call, which I
used at every reward. The Waiting-on line named who had to choose, every time. The Down message named
the card that caused it.

## Candidate issues

Engine mismatches (possible):

- Corrosive Acid's Turn Start Exhaust ended the run with Red holding eight cards, against section 9
  (note 7). Also the hand was discarded as part of going Down.

Rulebook questions:

- What does Exhaust do when the deck and the discard pile are both empty (note 7)?
- `Cap` and `Dim` are on room cards and not in the rulebook. Does Cap 3 count both sides' cards
  (note 2)?
- Is a player who cannot pay for any card in their hand meant to be locked out (note 5)?
- Should the rulebook say what happens to Bad Stuff held at Down?

Shape, independent of tuning:

- A stairwell can come second of nine rooms (note 1) or, as here, early with no clear (note 3).
- Flee gives Bad Stuff that cannot pay, so each Flee makes the next harder (What the run showed, 2).
- A Flee can be forced with no choice (turns 4, 6, 7).

Card observations for a later re-tune:

- Faceful Of Slime, Spore Cloud, Panic, Sluggish and Corrosive Acid cost 3, 3, 2, 2 and 3 to play,
  and the price of the cleanup was more than the hand could pay.
- Gears & Glitch's second line wanted both stats; neither hand could meet it without the other.
- Corrosive Acid with an empty deck ended the run in one Turn Start (note 7).

CLI:

- Reword the discard prompt (friction 1).
- Say why a hand has no legal play (friction 2).
- Print a note's log line when it is written (friction 3).
