# Playtest 14: Seed 39701

Recorded 2026-10-26. Kit revision R35.C59-v1 (rules R35; branch `claude/vibrant-gauss-fnuh4a` at
`a8859bb6`). Played by an agent standing in for a human playtester, in the CLI (`bin/nvu`), seed
39701, one shell call per decision. The run ended in **Defeat** on turn 12, on floor 2, during the
Outcome phase, when a Flee from the Dark Room made Gray go Down. Gray had 13 cards Exhausted, an empty
deck and discard pile, and 5 cards in hand. Red had 8 Exhausted and 12 live.

Notes are the agent's, verbatim, tagged `[agent]`, in the order they were written into the run.
Nothing here is a ruling. Suspected bugs were checked against `rulebook.md` and `cards.yaml` only;
the engine was not read.

## How the run went

Floor 1 took two turns. The first flip showed Puzzle Room, Tunnel of Rubble and Ventilation Shaft. I
entered the Shaft and cleared it on Oomph 4 and Scramble 4 (Charge paid by Shove and Overdrive,
Pick The Lock paid by two Peek Around Corners). I took Tag Team and Hack the Doors. The second flip
showed The Sentry Drone three times (note 1). I entered one, played Overdrive and Charge for Red and
Work The Angles for Gray's Oomph 1, and Ascended on Oomph 8. I took Fast Follow and Pivot. Everything
Exhausted healed on Ascend.

Floor 2 was lost without a clear attempt that Gray could take part in. Gray's deck is Pick The Lock
(4 Scramble, cost 2), Duck Under (2, cost 1) and a few cheap cards. With a hand of four or five,
paying for plays leaves almost no Scramble: 4 to 6 at best. Turn 3's Room 1-3 needed Scramble 5 and I
could reach 4 (note 2), so I Fled for Exhaust 2 each. Turn 4's flip showed an Ambush room, which
left me no choice (note 3), and its thresholds were out of reach, so I Fled for Bad Stuff: Red got
Panic and Gray got My Head Is Quantum Spinning. Turn 5 was the one clear of the floor: Maintenance on
Oomph 7 and Scramble 2 (Panic's +2 lifted the printed 0), three cards in the play zone, for a Reckless
Swing.

After that every room asked for Scramble 10 or more from Gray, or Oomph 12, or both stats at 8 and
above. I Fled turns 6 to 12 (note 5 onward). The stairwell, Gears & Glitch, was in the reveal five
times and I never had a hand that could take it. Each Flee added Bad Stuff: Spore Cloud, Corrosive
Acid, Torn Seal, System Feedback, a second Panic, a second Quantum Spinning. None of it can pay, so
Gray's hand grew to nine cards of which three could pay. Red shed Panic by discarding it to Spore Cloud
(note 8).

On turn 12 Gray's deck was empty and its discard had one card. Corrosive Acid's Turn Start Exhaust
used it up. I played Acid (cost 3, paid with Duck Under, Hack the Doors and a Pick The Lock) to
scrap it (note 11). The Dark Room Flee then said "Exhaust 1" to a Gray with no deck and no discard
pile and five cards in hand, and the run ended (note 12).

## What the run showed

1. **Gray cannot pay for the rooms the floor deck shows.** Gray's usable stat comes from Pick The Lock
   (cost 2 for 4) and Duck Under (cost 1 for 2), and a 5-card hand pays most of its Scramble back as
   cost. By hand count, 4 to 6 Scramble was the ceiling on every turn of floor 2, and the rooms I
   saw printed 7 to 13. Red's ceiling was about 7 Oomph, against 8 and 12 on the stairwells. Floor 1
   asked for 4 and 8, and I cleared both. The step between floors is large.
2. **A Flee makes the next Flee more likely.** Bad Stuff cannot pay, so each Flee shrinks the usable
   hand. Seven Flees in a row added nine Bad Stuff cards. The Ambush room (note 3) returned three
   times in 12 turns and its only legal result for me was a Flee with Bad Stuff.
3. **Down arrives by an Exhaust on an empty deck, not by an empty hand.** This is the second run in
   a row where a player with cards in hand was declared Down (playtest 13 note 7, this note 12).
   Both times the card or room only said "Exhaust". Whether intended or not, the rulebook's
   section 9 does not describe it.

## The CLI as a playtest tool

Friction, worst first:

1. The Spore Cloud discard prompt still reads "Discard down to 3 cards", then "choose 3 of". It does
   not say that the names are the cards to discard, and with two Clouds it listed four names for three
   discards. This is the same friction as playtest 13, still open.
2. I could not ask the tool whether a hand could reach a number. I counted payers by hand for every
   room on every turn, and I got one wrong (note 2). A "best reachable" line for each stat, with the
   hand as it stands, would stand in for that arithmetic.
3. After Ambush the CLI printed only one room to choose from, with no line saying why the other two
   were gone. The reveal still showed all three; the choose line then dropped to one.
4. `play note` prints the note text back and not its log line, so I found the lines with `replay`.
5. The Down message says "a room's printed punishment". It does not name the room or the missing
   cards, and it comes before the discards of the hand. The Corrosive Acid one in playtest 13 named
   the card.
6. I used an output redirect once by mistake, running `replay` with its output sent to the null
   device. It had no effect on the run or the note.

What worked: `play card` printed only what changed. Room faces printed the modified threshold as
"Scramble 10 (printed 8)", so I never had to add Panic by hand. The moves list showed the legal
payments, and left out Charge when it had no payers, which told me the rule was biting. The Waiting-on
line named who had to choose, every time. `bin/nvu card` gave a card's face in one call at each
reward.

## Candidate issues

Engine mismatches (possible):

- A Flee Exhaust on an empty deck and discard declared Gray Down with five cards in hand, against
  section 9 (note 12). Also seen in playtest 13 note 7. The hand is then discarded.

Rulebook questions:

- What does Exhaust do when the deck and the discard pile are both empty (note 12)?
- Are several copies of one room in a floor deck intended (notes 1 and 8)? The rulebook says the Roof
  has "only one Stairwell room" and is silent on floors.
- `Ambush` is on room cards and not in the rulebook. Should the other two revealed rooms still be
  ordered by the player (note 3)? Is it intended that an Ambush room hides the stairwell beside it
  (note 6)?
- Do two copies of one Holding effect both apply (note 10)? Does Spore Cloud's discard count another
  Spore Cloud (note 7)?
- Does `Dim` need a rulebook entry? I never saw its effect on a draw: Gray had 7 to 9 cards in hand.
- Does a threshold with only Oomph take a Scramble requirement from Panic? The card says so (note 5).

Shape, independent of tuning:

- A stairwell can come second of nine rooms and clear a floor in two turns (note 1).
- A player whose cheap cards are the only payers has a reach well below the room numbers (What the run
  showed, 1).
- Bad Stuff cannot pay, so each Flee shrinks the next hand (What the run showed, 2).
- An Ambush room can return repeatedly and leaves Flee as the only legal result (notes 4 and 6).

Card observations for a later re-tune:

- Floor 2 rooms print Scramble 7 to 13 and Gray's Scramble per card is 2 to 4 at cost 1 to 2.
- Gears & Glitch needs Oomph 12 or Oomph 7 and Scramble 7. Neither player held the cards for it once.
- Corrosive Acid with an empty deck ended the run in two turns (note 11).
- Spore Cloud and Torn Seal cost 3 to play, which is more than a spare hand holds.

CLI:

- Reword the discard prompt (friction 1).
- Show the best reachable stats for the hand (friction 2).
- Say why the room choice is a single room (friction 3).
- Print a note's log line when it is written (friction 4).
- Name the room and the missing cards in a Down message (friction 5).
