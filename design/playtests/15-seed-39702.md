# Playtest 15: Seed 39702

Recorded 2026-10-26. Kit revision R37.C60-v1 (rules R37; branch `claude/vibrant-gauss-fnuh4a` at
`4a1401f7`). Played by an agent standing in for a human playtester, in the CLI (`bin/nvu`), seed
39702, one shell call per decision. The run ended in **Defeat** on turn 22, on floor 3, during the
Play phase, when Red's Overdrive Exhausted Red's last card in hand, deck and discard pile. Red had
Coil Of Cable, Cross Punch and Overdrive in the play zone (Oomph 11, Scramble 10, enough for
Mercenaries' "Oomph 10 and Scramble 10") and 21 cards Exhausted. Gray had 2 live cards and 18
Exhausted.

Notes are the agent's, verbatim, tagged `[agent]`, in the order they were written into the run.
Nothing here is a ruling. Suspected bugs were checked against `rulebook.md` and `cards.yaml` only;
the engine was not read.

## How the run went

Floor 1 took seven turns and cleared well. Ventilation Shaft (turn 1) fell to Charge and Pick The
Lock. Security Turnstile (turn 2) fell to both of its single-stat challenges at once, so both
players got a reward. Puzzle Room (turn 3) has Lockstep; the CLI enforced it by dropping the
moves of whoever had just played, and I had Red play a Shove between Gray's two plays. Tunnel of
Rubble, Gross Thing and Regular Room...OF DOOM followed. By turn 6 Gray was on 4 live cards, because
every paid card is a card lost from the deck. Hit 'n Run (Shuffle a card from Exhaust into the
deck) and a free Coil Of Cable carried that turn. The floor deck then showed The Sentry Drone three
times in one reveal and nothing else (note 1). I entered one and Ascended on Oomph 8: Overdrive
Exhausted Red's last deck card, which turned on Bull Rush's +3. Both decks healed to 19 and 18 cards.

Floor 2 took four turns and went the same way. Regular Monster 2 (Ambush) was forced on turn 8; I
cleared it on Oomph 9 and Scramble 9 with Momentum Shift, Fast Follow, Bull Rush, Pick The Lock and
Hack the Doors. Room 1-3 (Scramble 5), Maintenance (Oomph 7 in two cards under Cap: I Know Kung Fu
for 5 and Overdrive for 3) and Gears & Glitch (Oomph 7 and Scramble 7, with a free Coil Of Cable
for 3) followed. Gears & Glitch appeared twice in one reveal on turn 11, as in playtest 14.

Floor 3 was lost. Crumbling Armory (turn 12) was the one clear, on Oomph 12 and Scramble 9, for a
Pry Bar and a Riot Shield from the Good Stuff and Momentum Shift and Zen Mode from the reward pools.
After it the floor deck held only Mercenaries, three Iron Sentinels, Scrap Pile, Slimy Bio-Hazard
Containment, Outrun The Laser and Gravity...Room. Every room asked for 15 of a stat, or 10 and 10,
or 8 and 8 under Heavy. The hands I held could not reach any of them (notes 4 and 6). I fled
eight turns in a row, each Flee costing 1 to 4 cards a side, and the decks fell from 22 and 21 to
7 and 6 live. Mercenaries and Scrap Pile traded places on top of and under the floor deck, because
Mercenaries is Relentless. I used Catch Your Breath on turn 16 to move three cards back to Gray's
deck. On turn 21 I tried Gravity...Room with Heavy, lost three deck cards to Heavy and cleared
nothing (note 7).

On turn 22 both discard piles had shuffled in and the hands were the best of the floor: Gray had Zen
Mode, two Pick The Lock and Riot Shield; Red had two Fast Follow, Overdrive, Coil Of Cable and Cross
Punch. Gray played Zen Mode and paid for both Scramble plays without Exhausting a card. I took
Zen Mode to cover Red as well and paid Cross Punch with both Fast Follows, which Exhausted them
(note 8). That left Overdrive. It Exhausted Red's last card and Red went Down with the room's
Oomph 10 and Scramble 10 already on the table (note 9).

## What the run showed

1. **The reveal decides the floor, and floor 3 had no door.** The floor 3 deck ran dry of rooms I
   could clear. After Crumbling Armory it held one 8-and-8 room under Heavy, two 15-of-one-stat
   rooms, a 14-Scramble room, Mercenaries, and three copies of a stairwell asking 10 and 10 or 18.
   I fled eight turns in a row and the cost of each Flee was paid in the same cards that I would
   have needed to clear. A Flee takes 1 to 4 cards a side and gives no card back. By turn 21 the
   decks were 7 and 6 live.
2. **Zen Mode and free cards are the real engine.** The only turn on floor 3 that came close to a
   second clear was turn 22, and it used a Zen Mode (Gray lost no cards on two plays), a free Coil Of
   Cable, and two free Fast Follows. Plays that cost no card (Coil, Overdrive, Pry Bar, Fast Follow
   after Gray plays) were what turned a 5-card hand into 10 of a stat. A hand of full-cost cards
   reached about 6 of a stat. This is a card observation, but it shapes what "reach" means (see
   notes 4 and 6).
3. **Down arrives from Exhaust on a nearly empty player.** Red went Down on a play, in the Play
   phase, from an Exhaust on the last card. This is the third run in a row that ended on a Down
   caused by an Exhaust (playtest 13 note 7, playtest 14 note 12, this note 9). In this run the
   rulebook's words match what the engine did.

## The CLI as a playtest tool

Friction, worst first:

1. `choose deck` for an Exhaust takes the top card of the deck blind, and the prompt does not say
   so. I took it for a choice of the deck as a group and lost Pick The Lock that way on turn 15
   (log 507). The rulebook says "the top card of your deck". A line "(top card, unseen)" would settle
   it.
2. I could not ask the tool whether a hand could reach a threshold. I counted payers by hand on every
   turn and misjudged Heavy (note 7). A line for the best reachable Oomph and Scramble with the
   current hand would replace that arithmetic. This repeats friction 2 of playtest 14.
3. `play note` prints the note text back and not its log line, so I found the lines with `replay`.
   This repeats friction 4 of playtest 14.
4. A card name with an apostrophe (`Hit 'n Run`) needs quotes in the shell. The first try failed with
   a shell error and not a CLI error. The CLI accepted the quoted name.
5. The Peek prompt for the Floor deck (note 3) does not say whether a discard exists. Only `order`
   is offered.
6. After a note is written on a finished run, the replay shows it but the CLI printed no sign that
   the run was closed. Minor.

What worked: the moves list printed every legal payment, and dropped moves that a rule had taken
away (Lockstep after a play, Charge when it had no payers). Costs showed as "cost 0 (printed 1)"
when Fast Follow's text changed them. Room faces printed a tick on the met threshold in the final
table. Each Exhaust prompt named who was choosing and how many were left, and offered hand, discard
and deck separately. `bin/nvu card` gave a card's face in one call at each reward. `play show` was
not needed on any turn.

## Candidate issues

Engine mismatches (possible):

- Peek on the Floor deck offered no discard step, against the Peek keyword in `rulebook.md` (note 3).

Rulebook questions:

- Does a floor deck, or a reward pool, hold several copies of one card (notes 1 and 2)? Three copies
  of one stairwell can make up a whole reveal.
- Who Exhausts when "the player who played the least Scramble" is a tie (note 5)?
- When is Down checked, and does the Outcome phase get to run first (note 9)? Do cards in the play
  zone count as live?
- Does Zen Mode cover only the player who played it (note 8)?
- Does Peek's "discard" step exist for the Floor deck (note 3)?

Shape, independent of tuning:

- A player whose hand cannot reach any room keeps the same hand and pays for it on every Flee (note
  6, What the run showed 1).
- A floor deck can run to a state where every revealed room is out of reach, with no way to rebuild
  the hand except by spending cards (What the run showed 1).
- Relentless puts a room on top, so a room that cannot be cleared is also the cheapest room to flee
  and is back next turn (note 4).
- Down can end a run in the middle of the Play phase with the thresholds already met (note 9).

Card observations for a later re-tune:

- Floor 3 rooms print 15 of one stat, 10 and 10, or 8 and 8 under Heavy. A hand of five full-cost
  cards reaches about 6 of a stat. Floor 2 asked for 7 to 10 and I cleared four of five rooms.
- Zen Mode, Coil Of Cable, Pry Bar and a free Fast Follow carried every large turn. Cards that cost
  nothing are the ones that scale.
- Heavy costs one deck card per play. Three plays cost three deck cards and the room was still out
  of reach.
- Outrun The Laser's Flee ("least Scramble Exhausts 3") needs a tie rule (note 5).
- Iron Sentinel's Flee is Exhaust 4 and Bad Stuff; I never entered it and chose Mercenaries,
  Scrap Pile and Gravity...Room instead.

CLI:

- Say that `choose deck` takes the top card, unseen (friction 1).
- Show the best reachable stats for the hand (friction 2).
- Print a note's log line when it is written (friction 3).
- Say what the Floor deck Peek can do (friction 5).
