# Playtest 16: Seed 39703

Recorded 2026-10-26. Kit revision R37.C60-v2 (rules R37; branch `claude/vibrant-gauss-fnuh4a` at
`e915b05f`). Played by an agent standing in for a human playtester, in the CLI (`bin/nvu`), seed
39703, one shell call per decision. The run ended in **Defeat** on turn 35, on floor 5, at
Outcome, when the Flee of Double Dark Hallway of Death had Red Exhaust 2 and Red's last two cards
(A Pair Of Stitch-Em-Ups and Pry Bar) were the two. Red had 29 cards Exhausted. Gray had 4 live
cards and 22 Exhausted. I cleared 14 rooms in all, five of them stairwells.

Notes are the agent's, verbatim, tagged `[agent]`, in the order they were written into the run.
Nothing here is a ruling. Suspected bugs were checked against `rulebook.md` and `cards.yaml` only;
the engine was not read.

## How the run went

Floor 1 took seven turns and went well. Security Turnstile (turn 1) fell to Overdrive and Shove for
Red and Pick The Lock and Duck Under for Gray, both single-stat challenges at once. Gross Thing,
Puzzle Room (Lockstep; I alternated Red and Gray plays and got Oomph 8 for Good Stuff), Ventilation
Shaft, Tunnel of Rubble and Regular Room...OF DOOM followed. By turn 4 the decks were down to 9 and
6 live, because every payer is a lost card. A Pair Of Stitch-Em-Ups (from the turn 3 Good Stuff)
moved two Pick The Lock back into Gray's deck. The floor 1 deck held three copies of The Sentry
Drone, so turn 7 revealed three Drones and nothing else (note 4). Gray's I Know Kung Fu (Oomph 5)
and Red's Pry Bar (Oomph 3) made Oomph 8 for one paid card.

Floor 2 took four turns. The first reveal had Gears & Glitch twice (note 6). Automated Defense
Turret fell to Scramble 8 (Pick The Lock, Tag Team, Duck Under) for two Good Stuff. Regular Monster 2
(Ambush) fell on Oomph 11 and Scramble 9; Zen Mode let Gray play Pick The Lock and Riot Shield with
no card lost, and Cross Punch gave Red 8 Oomph with the Scramble bonus. Room 1-3 cleared on
Scramble 5 only. Gears & Glitch fell on exactly Oomph 7 and Scramble 7 with two free cards (Pry Bar,
Fast Follow) and a free Coil Of Cable.

Floor 3 took one turn. The Iron Sentinel (Oomph 10 and Scramble 10) fell on the first try: Zen Mode,
Coil Of Cable, Riot Shield and two Duck Unders for Gray, Tag Team, Fast Follow and two Overdrives
for Red.

Floor 4 took eight turns, and the stairwell was the whole floor. Sexy Blob (Ambush, Relentless,
Oomph 20 or Scramble 20) was forced in on the first reveal. I fled it on turns 13, 14 and 17.
Relentless put it back on top each time (note 7). On turn 15 it was revealed beside Regular Monster 4,
also Ambush, so I chose Monster 4 and Blob went to the bottom (note 8). On turn 17 I reached 19 of
20 Oomph (note 9). On turn 20 I reached 20. Red played two Pry Bars for 6; Gray played Zen Mode,
I Know Kung Fu (5), Synergy Link and Hit 'n Run (1 Oomph) for 6; the draws from Kung Fu and Synergy
found Hit 'n Run; and Cross Punch, with the Scramble bonus, added 8. Both players got two Good Stuff
and Ascended. The decks went back to 28 cards each.

Floor 5 was lost over 15 turns. The floor deck held seven rooms. Six of them asked for 15, 18 or
20 of a stat, or 15 and 15 (two of those under Heavy). The seventh, Bubbly Slime Monster, asked for
Oomph 8 and Scramble 8 under Corrosive and was the only clear. The stairwell, Comrade Black (Oomph 25
and Scramble 16, Heavy), came up beside the Ambush room Monster 5-3 on every reveal where I saw
it, so I never could enter it. I fled 14 turns. Each Flee cost 2 a side. The decks fell from 28 and
28 to 0 and 4. Catch Your Breath moved three cards back for Gray twice. On turn 35 Red held two
cards and the Flee asked for two.

## What the run showed

1. **A stairwell that must be entered and cannot be skipped is a hard wall.** Sexy Blob took four
   visits (turns 13, 14, 17, 20) and the cheapest Flees I made still cost 2 a side plus Bad Stuff.
   The 20 Oomph was met with no spare point, using Zen Mode (free payments), two free Pry Bars,
   Kung Fu's draws and a Cross Punch with its Scramble bonus. Without Zen Mode, or without the
   Scramble card to trigger Cross Punch, I do not see a route. Notes 7 and 9.
2. **Floor 5 had no door I could open.** Six of the seven rooms asked 15, 18 or 20 of a stat, or
   15 and 15 (two under Heavy) and the stairwell asked 25 and 16 under Heavy. The room I cleared
   asked 8 and 8. From turn 21 on, all 15 turns were Flee turns but one, each costing 2 to 3 cards a
   side. The decks fell 28, 28 to 0, 4. This repeats the shape of playtest 15 on floor 3: a run
   that arrives on a late floor with a deck of 28 cannot afford the cost of looking for the one
   hand that works.
3. **Cards that cost nothing carry the turns.** Every big turn used Pry Bar, Coil Of Cable, Fast
   Follow (free after Gray plays), Zen Mode (free payments for Gray), Riot Shield (returns on a
   Clear) or Cross Punch's bonus. Plain starter cards (Shove, Charge, Duck Under) were mostly
   payers. This is the same note as playtest 15 What the run showed 2.

## The CLI as a playtest tool

Friction, worst first:

1. A multi-word card name needs shell quotes in `pay` and `choose`, and the refusal is hard to
   read. `card Red Shove pay Lean In` was refused with `"In" matches nothing eligible. Candidates:
   Shove.` It read "Lean" as nothing and "In" as a second payer. `choose "discard Shove"` was
   refused as well, and `choose discard Shove` (three words, no quotes) worked. The Exhaust prompt
   says `Write "hand <Name>" or "discard <Name>"`, which reads as quotes.
2. I could not ask whether a hand could reach a threshold. On turn 17 I counted payers and
   draws by hand for the Blob and was one Oomph short. This repeats friction 2 of playtest 15.
3. `play note` prints the note back and not its log line, so I found the lines with `replay`.
   This repeats friction 3 of playtest 15.
4. The Exhaust prompt for the top of the deck now says "unseen" (the fix from the last commit), but
   I still lost two Overdrives, a Zen Mode and a Pry Bar to it when I chose it for speed. That
   is the rule, and the prompt is clear. A sentence of what is in the deck (a count by card name)
   would let a player choose the deck on purpose.
5. "Red shuffles 17 cards back in" (log 268) read as the whole deck, but Red's deck was 19. The
   line counts only the cards moved, not the deck cards already there. Gray's line said 18 for 19.
6. The Corrosive log line says "Exhausting them" for cards that were Scrapped (note 11).
7. The room faces change with Panic and Sluggish ("Oomph 20 and Scramble 2 (printed 0)", "cost 3
   (printed 2)"), which is good, but the same face also appears in the Revealed list before I
   enter, so I read the Panic penalty into rooms I had not entered yet. It is correct; it is also
   only obvious in the printed line.
8. Long Flee turns print the full table after each prompt, which hid the next prompt. I cut it
   with `tail` and `head`. A `--quiet` for `play` would help.

What worked: the moves list printed every legal payment and dropped the ones a rule had taken
away (Lockstep after a play, a cost 0 Fast Follow after Gray played). Every Exhaust, draw,
Scrap and reward prompt named who was choosing and how many were left. `bin/nvu card` gave a face
in one call at each reward. Ambush with two rooms printed both options. The `Catch Your Breath`
prompt listed the Exhaust pile in full. Undo was not needed.

## Candidate issues

Engine mismatches (possible):

- None found. The Corrosive log line says "Exhausting" for a Scrap (note 11); the pile counts match
  the rulebook, so this is wording only.

Rulebook questions:

- Does a floor deck, a reward pool or a Stuff pool hold copies of one card (notes 1 to 6)? On
  floor 1 a third of the floor was one stairwell. Reveals of three identical rooms leave no choice.
- Is an Ambush plus Relentless stairwell a loop by design, and is a second Ambush room in the
  reveal the intended exit (notes 7 and 8)?
- Should a threshold with no printed Scramble gain Scramble from Panic (note 8)?
- Should Bad Stuff return to its pool at an Ascend, or stay in the shuffle (note 10)?
- Carried from earlier notes: when Down is checked (playtest 15 note 9). Here Red went Down at
  Outcome in the middle of a Flee Exhaust (log 1061), with nothing in the play zone, so this run
  does not add to that question.
- When does Catch Your Breath resolve? It moved cards at Outcome, after "You Flee" and before the
  room's Exhausts (logs 572 to 577). The card says only "If this is the only card you play this
  turn". The rulebook has no timing for a card that checks the whole turn.
- Does Zen Mode stop the Exhaust from a Flee? I never tested it. Playtest 15 note 8 left it open,
  and I played Zen Mode on turn 35 for that purpose; Red went Down first.

Shape, independent of tuning:

- A late floor with every room at 15 or more of a stat leaves only Flee turns, and each Flee turn
  costs the cards that a clear would need (What the run showed 2).
- A forced room that returns each turn (note 7) turns a floor into a countdown.
- The Flee exhaust prompts offer the deck blind or a hand card, so a Flee is also a hand reset.
  I used the hand picks to cycle weak cards, which kept the hand from freezing. It worked, and
  it is a hidden rule of thumb.

Card observations for a later re-tune:

- Sexy Blob (20), Comrade Black (25 and 16, Heavy) and floor 5's 15 and 18 rooms are far above
  anything the floor 4 reward cards support.
- Cross Punch (Oomph 4, +4 with a Scramble card first) and I Know Kung Fu (Oomph 5 for 1) were
  the two cards that made 20 Oomph reachable.
- Catch Your Breath is the only heal that costs no card, and the only place I saw it fit was a
  turn where Gray played nothing else.
- Zen Mode, Coil Of Cable, Pry Bar, Riot Shield and Fast Follow carried every large turn.
- Bad Stuff that cannot pay (System Feedback, Sluggish, Panic, Rust) cost a hand slot each and
  were cheaper to Flee-exhaust than to play; Sluggish and Rust did not come into play.

CLI:

- Make the quote rule visible, or accept `choose discard Shove` and `choose "discard Shove"` both
  (friction 1).
- Show the best reachable stats for the hand (friction 2).
- Print a note's log line when it is written (friction 3).
- Say what the shuffle-back count means (friction 5) and say "Scrapped" for Corrosive (friction 6).
