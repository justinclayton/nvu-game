# Playtest 17: Seed 40701

Recorded 2026-11-27. Kit revision R38.C63-v4 (rules R38; branch `pay-by-discard` at `6ebc176a`).
Played by an agent standing in for a human playtester, in the CLI (`bin/nvu`), seed 40701, one
shell call per decision. The run ended in **Defeat** on turn 30, on floor 5, at Outcome, when the
Flee of Comrade Black had Gray Exhaust their last card (Pick The Lock). Red had 3 cards left and 25
Exhausted. Gray had 0 and 25 Exhausted. I cleared 14 rooms, four of them stairwells. Floors 1 to 4
took 14 turns. Floor 5 took 16 turns and I cleared one room on it.

This is the first playtest on R38, where a paid card goes to the discard pile and not to the
Exhaust pile.

Notes are the agent's, verbatim, tagged `[agent]`, in the order they were written into the run.
Nothing here is a ruling. Suspected bugs were checked against `rulebook.md` and `cards.yaml` only;
the engine was not read.

## How the run went

Floor 1 took seven turns and went smoothly. Tunnel of Rubble, Gross Thing, Regular Room...OF DOOM,
Security Turnstile and Ventilation Shaft all fell to Oomph 4 to 5 and Scramble 4 to 5. Paying was
cheap: a payer goes to the discard pile and comes back at the next shuffle, so I paid freely and
only chose Overdrive when I wanted its Exhaust. Puzzle Room (Lockstep) needed Red and Gray to
alternate. I did that with Charge, Pick The Lock, Tag Team, Hack the Doors and Overdrive for Oomph 9.
The Sentry Drone came last as the only room left, was forced, and fell to Cutting Torch and
Overdrive for Oomph 8. The floor had seven rooms and no repeated room.

Floor 2 took three turns. Regular Monster 2 (Ambush) fell on Oomph 9 and Scramble 10: Tag Team drew
both players a card, Overdrive and Charge gave Oomph, and two Pick The Locks gave Scramble. Room 1-3
fell on Oomph 4 and Scramble 7. Gears & Glitch fell on Oomph 12: I Know Kung Fu, Momentum Shift and
Overdrive. Floor 3 took one turn. The Iron Sentinel (Oomph 10 and Scramble 10) fell to Overdrive,
Bull Rush and Brute Recycle (Oomph 11, Scramble 2) plus two Pick The Locks for Gray. Brute Recycle
Scrapped a Charge.

Floor 4 took three turns. The first reveal had two Ambush rooms, Regular Monster 4 and Sexy Blob.
I entered Monster 4, saw that Oomph 8 and Scramble 12 was out of reach, and fled it for Exhaust 1
(note 2). Turn 13 was Oasis of Doom at Oomph 10 and Scramble 10. Tag Team drew a Fast Follow, which
was then free, and In Step scored 2 per Red play (four plays, so 8). Turn 14 forced Sexy Blob (Ambush,
Relentless, Oomph 20 or Scramble 20) and I cleared it at once. Gray played Quick Vault, two I Know
Kung Fus, Hit 'n Run and Work The Angles. Each Scramble card played drew two cards, so Gray saw
nine cards and found Pivot and Synergy Link. Red played Cutting Torch and Brute Recycle for exactly
Oomph 20. Both players got two Good Stuff each and Ascended.

Floor 5 was the wall. Its seven rooms were Bubbly Slime Monster (Oomph 8 and Scramble 8, Corrosive),
The Authorities (15 or 15 and 15), Monster 5-3 (Ambush, Heavy, 15 and 15), Monster 5-2 (18 and 18),
Monster 5-4 (18 and 18, or Scramble 12 with Draining), Double Dark Hallway of Death (20) and the
stairwell Comrade Black (Oomph 25 and Scramble 16, Heavy). I cleared Bubbly Slime Monster on turn 17,
and it cost Gray four Scrapped cards, because Corrosive Scraps what you pay with. Every other turn I
fled. Because a fled room goes to the bottom, the deck of six rooms turned in a fixed loop. Monster
5-3 came up on every second reveal, was Ambush, and had to be entered each time (note 3). Each Flee
cost both players 2 cards, or 1 plus a Bad Stuff for Comrade Black. On 12 of the 16 floor 5 turns I
played no card at all. Gray went Down on turn 30.

## Notes

1. **Peek has no discard step.** `[agent]` (log 212)

   > Hack the Doors says Peek 3, and the rulebook's Peek says 'Discard any number of them, then put the rest back in any order.' The CLI only asked me to order the 3 Good Stuff cards, with no way to discard any. Expected a discard choice. Either the CLI skips it or Peek in this build is reorder only; question for the designer.

   `rulebook.md` Keywords: "`Peek X`: Reveal the top **X** cards of ANY face-down pile. Discard any
   number of them, then put the rest back in any order." `cards.yaml` gives Hack the Doors the text
   "Peek 3". The CLI printed "A look at the Good Stuff pool: Riot Shield, Cutting Torch, Stim Pack"
   and then "Put those 3 back on top, in the order you choose." There was no prompt to discard. The
   next reveal (logs 219 and 220) showed the same three cards in the order I gave. Possible engine
   mismatch, or the rulebook line is out of date. I do not know which, so it is a question for the
   designer. Also: "discard" from a Stuff pool has no stated destination in the rulebook.

2. **A cheap Flee keeps the hand.** `[agent]` (log 459)

   > Floor 4 turn 12: Regular Monster 4 (Oomph 8 and Scramble 12, or Oomph 20) is out of reach for this 5-card hand, and its Flee is only Exhaust 1. I am fleeing on purpose instead of paying cards for an impossible clear. Since I hold 5 cards I will draw nothing next turn, so the Flee is a pure room reroll that also keeps my hand. Question: is a cheap Flee that keeps the hand meant to be a free reroll?

   `rulebook.md` Start of Turn: "Draw up to five... If you already hold 5 or more, do not draw." and
   Outcome: "**Flee**: ... Resolve the `Flee:` outcome ... then **put the room card on the bottom of
   the Floor deck**." So the log is as written (logs 460 to 467). Both players Exhausted one card
   (Shove and Work The Angles, my two weakest) and kept the other four. The next reveal gave
   Oasis of Doom, which I cleared. A Flee for Exhaust 1 therefore cost one weak card and rerolled
   the room. Question for the designer: is that intended? On floor 5 the same move cost 2 a side
   and was no longer cheap.

## Unnoted observations

I did not write these into the run, so they have no log line. They come from reading the replay.

- **At Turn Start timing (log 837).** Gray held Corrosive Acid ("Holding: At Turn Start, Exhaust
  1"). The Exhaust prompt appeared after "Play phase starts" (log 836), not before "Turn Start
  phase ends" (log 835). It blocked `end` until answered. `rulebook.md` has no timing for "At
  Turn Start" on a Holding card. Question for the designer: should that Exhaust resolve inside the
  Turn Start phase?
- **Down mid-Flee (log 943).** The Comrade Black Flee reads "Both players Exhaust 1, and both
  players get Bad Stuff." Gray's last card was Exhausted (log 942) and the run ended ("Gray is
  Down", log 943) before Gray got the Bad Stuff. `rulebook.md` 9: "If one player has no cards in
  their hand, deck, or discard pile, you go `Down`." A Bad Stuff card in hand counts as a card,
  as the live count showed on turn 22 (logs 777 to 783: Red held 12 before and after
  "Exhaust 1 and gain Bad Stuff"). So the order inside the Flee decided the game. Question: is
  Down checked the moment the last card goes, or when the outcome finishes? This is the third
  playtest to touch when Down is checked (playtest 15 note 9, playtest 16 candidate issues).
- **Bad Stuff counts as stamina.** The same log lines show a stairwell Flee costs net 0 live
  cards if the Exhaust takes a card that is not a Bad Stuff already held and Bad Stuff comes in,
  or net -1 to -2 otherwise. The rulebook says "Your deck is your HP" and puts Bad Stuff in
  the hand. Question: should Bad Stuff in hand count toward staying alive?
- **Brute Recycle's Scrap is mandatory.** At log 415 and 541 the prompt "Scrap which starter card
  from your hand or discard pile?" offered no "none". The card says "Scrap 1 starter card from
  your hand or discard pile. If you do, you may draw 1 card." The rulebook's "may Scrap" wording is
  for optional Scraps. Question: is "If you do" about having no starter to Scrap, or is the Scrap
  optional?
- **Challenge boundaries are not printed.** Gross Thing ("Oomph 5: Red gets Good Stuff." and
  "Scramble 5: Gray reveals a card reward.") resolved both lines (logs 62 and 63). Regular Monster 2
  ("Scramble 10" and "Oomph 8 and Scramble 8") resolved only the second, with Oomph 9 and
  Scramble 10 both met (log 300). That matches `rulebook.md` 3 if the two Monster 2 lines are one
  challenge and the two Gross Thing lines are two. The face prints no divider, so a player cannot
  tell. I worked it out from outcomes, as in earlier playtests.
- **Duplicates.** Floors had seven rooms and no repeated room (playtest 16 had nine rooms and
  repeats). Reward reveals still held copies: Quick Vault twice (log 104), Momentum Shift twice
  (log 139), Junk Launcher twice (log 28). Good Stuff reveals showed Cutting Torch after Red already
  held one (log 546) and Duct Tape & Wire twice across two reveals (logs 547 and 553). The question
  from playtests 15 and 16 on copies in pools stands.

## What the run showed

1. **Paying is cheap now, and the cost moved to Exhaust.** Floors 1 to 4 went in 14 turns, and
   my decks went from 12 to 19, 22 and 24 cards by adding a card per room. A payer returns at the
   next shuffle, so I paid with whatever was in hand and the turn count stayed low. The cards I
   lost were the ones I chose to Exhaust, Overdrive's deck Exhaust, and Flee Exhausts. Free cards
   (Pry Bar, Coil Of Cable, Overdrive, Fast Follow after Gray plays, Quick Vault) and draw engines
   (I Know Kung Fu, Tag Team, Hit 'n Run, Work The Angles) carried every big turn: Sexy Blob fell in
   one turn on 9 Gray cards seen. That is worth testing against the old pay-by-Exhaust runs once
   cards are set.
2. **Floor 5 is a closed loop.** Of seven rooms, one was reachable (8 and 8). The others asked 15
   to 25 of a stat, so every turn after the clear was a Flee, with 12 of 16 floor 5 turns having no
   plays. A Flee costs 2 a side and cannot be undone until the floor ends, so Down was a matter of
   counting: Red 23 and Gray 24 on entry, 0 and 3 plus Exhausts by turn 30. The rooms rotate in a
   fixed order, so a forced room recurs every second turn (note 3). This repeats playtests 15 and
   16 (a late floor with every room at 15 or more of a stat).
3. **Down is a timing question again.** The game ended inside a Flee, before the outcome's Bad
   Stuff arrived, and Bad Stuff in hand counts as a card. Two rules the rulebook states plainly
   (9 and Stuff goes to the hand) interact in a way it does not state.

## The CLI as a playtest tool

Friction, worst first:

1. A pending prompt does not stop a chained command from being tried. On turn 25 Corrosive Acid's
   Turn Start Exhaust was pending, so my `end` and the next four `choose` calls were refused or
   landed on the wrong question. By luck the last accepted one named the Acid. The refusal text
   prints the prompt, so nothing was lost, but I had chained five calls. A `--stop-on-prompt` or
   a one-line "waiting on" at the top of every output would help.
2. Output after `flip`, `end` and each Flee Exhaust reprints the whole table. A 16-turn Flee loop
   was about 40 pages of output. A `--quiet` for `play` would help (the same ask as playtest 16
   friction 8).
3. `play note` prints the note back and not its log line. I found the lines with `replay`
   (the same ask as playtests 15 and 16).
4. I could not ask what a hand could reach. I counted by hand on turns 11, 13, 14 and 15 (the
   same ask as playtests 15 and 16, friction 2).
5. The log does not say which zone an Exhausted card came from. At log 735 "Red Exhausts Fast
   Follow" does not say hand or discard, and both held one. `choose Fast Follow` was accepted
   with no zone, and I do not know which one it took. The prompt says to write `hand <Name>` or
   `discard <Name>` when both hold one; it did not refuse, so I assume it picked one.
6. A multi-word card needs quotes in `choose`, and `choose "hand In Step"` and
   `choose "discard Peek Around Corner"` as one quoted argument worked. This is easy to get wrong
   (same as playtest 16 friction 1).
7. A move list that drops an unpayable card (Cross Punch vanished when Red held one other card)
   is correct but unexplained. I learned why by counting.
8. "Red shuffles 15 cards back in" at log 264 counts the cards moved, not the new deck (19). Same
   as playtest 16 friction 5.
9. The shell did not refuse an output redirect. I used one by mistake in a `card` call
   (`>/dev/null 2>&1`) before I caught it. It did nothing to the game. A pipe in a `grep` call was
   refused. I wrote nothing outside this note.

What worked: the Peek, Exhaust, draw, Scrap and reward prompts each named who was choosing and
what the options were. `Corrosive` printed "Scrapping them", fixing the wording from playtest 16.
Sluggish and Panic showed on the card as "cost 3 (printed 2)" and "Scramble 17 (printed 15)".
`In Step` printed "Scramble 4 now". `bin/nvu card` gave a face in one call at each reward. Undo
was not needed.

## Candidate issues

Engine mismatches (possible):

- Peek offers no discard step (note 1; `rulebook.md` "`Peek X`").
- A holding "At Turn Start" Exhaust resolved in the Play phase (unnoted observations, log 837).
  Possibly a rulebook gap rather than a mismatch.

Rulebook questions:

- When is Down checked, and does Bad Stuff owed by the same outcome count (log 943; also
  playtest 15 note 9)?
- Does Bad Stuff in hand count as a card for staying alive (logs 777 to 783)?
- Is "If you do" on Brute Recycle's Scrap optional, and should the prompt offer "none"?
- Is a cheap Flee that keeps the hand meant to be a free reroll (note 2)?
- Is a late-floor Ambush room at 15 and 15 under Heavy meant to be a wall (note 3)?
- How is the discard step of Peek meant to work for a Stuff pool or a reward pool?
- Do pools hold copies of a card (playtests 15 and 16, here logs 28, 104, 139, 546, 553)?
- Where does a card face show where one challenge ends and the next begins?

Shape, independent of tuning:

- Floor 5 had one reachable room, so 14 of 16 turns were Flee turns (note 3, What the run showed 2).
- Free cards and draw engines carry the big turns; plain starter cards are payers.
- A fixed-order floor deck with a forced Ambush room recurs every second turn once the other rooms
  are unreachable.

Card observations for a later re-tune:

- Comrade Black (Oomph 25 and Scramble 16, Heavy) and floor 5's 15, 18 and 20 rooms sit far above
  what floors 1 to 4 reward cards give.
- I Know Kung Fu, Hit 'n Run and Work The Angles together drew 4 on one play of each Scramble card
  (log 531 to 536). Quick Vault plus them found nine cards in one turn.
- In Step scales with Red plays, so Red playing four cheap cards made it 8 Scramble for 2 cards.
- Overdrive (3 Oomph for no payer, Exhaust 1 from the deck) is a free 3 Oomph. Under R38 it is the
  main reason a payer is not needed.
- Corrosive (Bubbly Slime Monster) cost Gray four cards Scrapped for one 8 and 8 clear.

CLI:

- Stop chained commands on a pending prompt, and print the waiting line first (friction 1).
- A `--quiet` for `play` (friction 2).
- Print a note's log line when it is written (friction 3).
- Say which zone an Exhaust took a card from (friction 5).
