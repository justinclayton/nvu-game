North vs Up — exemplar cards, ticket 12

Print at 100% (no “fit to page”) and cut on the borders — cards are 63×88mm, standard poker size, so they fit ordinary sleeves. Border colour is rarity, which signals a card's value — its ceiling when things line up. The most complex cards are always the highest rarity, because complexity has to pay for itself in what the card does. Every number here is a placeholder for ticket 10. Nothing on this sheet is adopted design — see design/core-design/prototypes/12-exemplar-card-set.md.

Stuff rooms — the room and the item are different cards

Each line is read against one character's own side of the play zone, never the shared pool, so you pay for your own item and never for your partner's. The lines are not addressed to Red or Gray: the room prints a Power line and a Scramble line, and the pair decide between them who takes which, one line each. Every threshold number on these four cards is a placeholder — how low the gate sits, and what the second tier costs, are ticket 10 numbers and are not decided. The room is Cleared whether or not anyone meets anything; what you lose by meeting nothing is only the Stuff. What you earn is drawn face down from the Good Stuff pool.
