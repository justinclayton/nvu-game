North vs Up — exemplar cards, ticket 12

Print at 100% (no “fit to page”) and cut on the borders — cards are 63×88mm, standard poker size, so they fit ordinary sleeves. Border colour is rarity, which signals how hard a card is to read and never how good it is. Every number here is a placeholder for ticket 10. Nothing on this sheet is adopted design — see design/core-design/prototypes/12-exemplar-card-set.md.
