North vs Up — the card list (print & cut)

Print at 100% (no “fit to page”) and cut on the borders — cards are 63×88mm, standard poker size, so they fit ordinary sleeves. The bar across the top is the card's type — Red, Gray, green for Good Stuff, plum for Bad Stuff, black for floor cards. The title colour is rarity, which signals a card's value — its ceiling when things line up. The most complex cards are always the highest rarity, because complexity has to pay for itself in what the card does. Every number here is a placeholder for ticket 10. Every card here is rendered from design/cards.yaml, the one place a card is written down; the design reasoning lives in design/core-design/prototypes/12-exemplar-card-set.md and 12-card-bank.md.
