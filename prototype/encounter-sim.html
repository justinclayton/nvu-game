What this implements, and where it is guessing

Everything below marked stub is a placeholder for an open ticket, not a decision. Everything else is the rules as written today.

The turn (ticket 07)

  1. Flip — a room from the floor deck into the active room zone. If the draw pile is empty, the Fled pile shuffles back in first.
  2. Draw — both characters at once, one card at a time, each stopping when they declare they are done. You cannot draw again once the play phase begins.
  3. Play — either character, any order. Paying a cost exhausts that many other cards from that character's hand. The room is Cleared the instant the shared pool meets its challenge; excess evaporates.
  4. Cleanup — if the room is still there, take its punishment and send it to Fled. Then exhaust every card in hand and in the play zone, except Hold cards still in hand.
  5. Turn end.

Last stand (ticket 17)

The first time a character's deck becomes empty — by drawing its last card or by a punishment taking it — every card in their hand is free to play for the rest of that turn. If the room is Cleared before that character goes down, they survive: at that cleanup, the cards that would have been exhausted are shuffled back into their deck instead, and they carry on with a thin but now-known deck. Once per character per floor.

  • Trigger fires when the deck becomes empty, not when a draw from an empty deck is attempted. agent reading
  • Hold cards in hand stay in hand — they were never going to be exhausted. agent reading
  • Only the character in last stand plays free; their partner pays costs normally. agent reading
  • A last stand triggered by a cleanup punishment gives nothing that turn — the room was not cleared, so there is no free play left to use and no survival. agent reading
