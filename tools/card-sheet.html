North vs Up — the card sheet, double-sided

Print on US Letter at 100% (no “fit to page”), two-sided, flip on long edge. Each front page is followed by its back page, mirrored, so the backs land behind their fronts.

Cut along the crop marks on the front side; a back that drifts a millimetre is white on white. If your printer offsets the back consistently, add ?shift=x,y (millimetres) to the address to nudge every back page. ?sheet=N prints one sheet alone.
